Armor

Note: Stat boosts and immunities are more meaningful end-game than simple defensive upgrades. Hold onto equipment that grants elemental immunities and stat boosts. Of particular note are the Dragon Boots, Fire Shield and Ice Shield, which are effectively end-game equipment because of their stats and/or resistances.

Inventory Items

Note: Numerous items can be missed forever when the player uses the X-Plane Talon unit. This even includes some very useful Talon units, like the FlushX and the Berth, the latter of which allows you to remove transformations, the former lets you recover and rest for free aboard the Talon. You can also miss crystals if you are not careful, reducing the number of Lost Spells and epic items you can craft.

Weapons

Note: Many powerful enemies have the ODamage trait, making normal weapons far less effective against them. For the last 3rd of the game, weapons which cannot pierce ODamage are not advised. Also note that many weapons cast magic spells when used from the inventory. This is very useful for mutants when a player is trying to conserve magic points in large dungeons. Finally, many races have preferred weapons, and they gain a special damage multiplier when using them.

Magic Spells

Note: Make sure you have a complete collection of magic on every character before you warp to Pureland. Many spells become unavailable after that point. Also note that you will only have enough crystals to create one of each Lost Magic spells. If you want duplicates, it will cost you at least one of your spells. Additionally, Type-less damage spells ignore enemy resistances - which can both help and hurt you. Finally, the Morph Lost Magic spell can be missed if you don't speak with Granny at the Inn in Elan during the future.

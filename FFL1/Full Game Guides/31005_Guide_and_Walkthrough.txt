Section 1: Introduction

Hello, and welcome to my Final Fantasy Legend 1 walkthrough. Thanks for looking
in.

I guess the first thing to note is, if one was unaware, is, yes, this isn't a
"real" Final Fantasy game. Its name in Japan is "MakaiToushi SaGa" (which
definitely doesn't translate to anything to do with Final Fantasy). It also has
two followups for Gameboy, Final Fantasy Legend 2 and 3 (which, again, aren't
"real" Final Fantasy games). On the other hand, who cares what they're called
or what they're considered, it's a fun game, and that's the only thing that
matters.

Notes about this walkthrough. The section headings are written the same in the
table of contents as they are in the walkthrough, so, you can just search for
the phrase (that is, search for "3.05: Castle of Sheild" or something), and
you'll be able to jump right to the section. Everything's broken down pretty
well, so, you should be able to find what you're looking for fairly easily.

I don't want to say this walkthrough is spoiler-free, because it isn't, it does
contain spoilers of sorts, depending on what one considers a spoiler. On the
other hand, I've done my best not to mention story points unless it's critical
to advancing. That is, I'll tell you that, for instance, you'll "walk forward
and talk to a girl," but won't really say who or what she's telling you unless
it's needed for you to advance. This walkthrough is a guide to help you find
your way along, not tell you everything that's happening (so if you're hoping
to just skip to the end and find out about the last boss and the ending, you're
not going to find it). So, as I said, it's not spoiler-free, but, I've done my
best to keep it to a minimum.

That said, go out there and explore, and thanks for stopping by here when you
get stuck or need some help.

3.01: Starting out (World 1, Floor 1)

Upon starting up a new game, you will first be presented with the story:

---

  It has been said
 that the tower in
 the center of the
 World is connected
     to Paradise.
Dreaming of a life
    in Paradise,
many have challenged
  the secret of the
       tower,
  but no one knows
what became of them.
        Now,
there is another who
   will brave the
     adventure

---

From there, you're presented with the first character creation screen. Your
choices are:

1: Human (M)
2: Human (F)
3: Mutant (M)
4: Mutant (F)
5: Clipper
6: Redbull
7: Wererat
8: Zombie

Pick your first character, and name it (4 characters max for name) and press
Start.

After finishing that, your character is placed on the outskirts of a tower.
From there, head north and up the staircase into the inn. Then, go to the
counter on the right and talk to the man. The Guild menu will open up, and your
character list will appear with a hand pointing at the character you created.
Here, you can make 3 more characters for your party. So, move the hand down to
one of the empty spots on the list and press A to open up the character
creation list again. Make another character as you did the first time. Do this
twice more to complete your group of four.

Now, you can take some time to explore the town, Base Town, if you want. The
counter next to the guild is the Inn counter. Obviously you don't need an inn
now, but, once you've gone outside and had some battles and lost health, you
can go to the inn to regain health. The cost of staying at the inn is 1GP per
unit of health being healed (that is, if the four of your characters added
together have lost, say, 35 cumulative HPs, the cost of staying at the inn will
be 35GP).

To the right of the guild counter, you'll see a bridge leading to a door. Going
up to it, you'll find out that the door is "locked by magic of black".
Unlocking this door is your main task in this area, but, you won't be getting
to that for a while.

To the right and up from that door is a counter with two people behind it,
selling weapons and items. At the weapon shop here, for sale is:

Long(Sword): 50: 132GP
Axe: 50: 412GP
Rapier: 50: 24GP
Gold(Gauntlet): 150GP
Gold(Armor): 300GP
Ice(Spell): 20: 500GP
Elec(Spell): 20: 500GP

At the item shop, for sale is:

Strong: 1: 300GP
Agility: 1: 300GP
HP200: 1: 100
Potion: 3: 50GP
XPotion: 3: 200GP
Antidote: 3: 100GP
Eyedrop: 3: 500GP
Revive: 1: 15000GP
(Heart): 1: 10000GP

Again, obviously you have no money now so you can't buy any of this, but, it's
there for you to come back to later.

Also, behind the building, on the left side and out back is a person standing
by a wall. Talking to them will bring up the House of Life menu. Here, you can
revive dead party members for 100GP. Be careful though, because each time you
revive someone it uses up one of their hearts, and once they're out of hearts
they can't be revived again. You can buy hearts at shops, but, they're very
expensive. Best to avoid dying of course, but, if you need it, it's there.

The last thing of note in the town is, in the back of the inn by the beds is a
person wandering around. Talk to him, and he'll tell you "Gen-Bu has hidden the
key to the door in the Statue of Hero." Sounds like a good hint.

Once you're done exploring the town, walk to the outside edge and out onto the
gravel area to exit the town.

3.02: Town of Hero

Now you'll find yourself outside on the world map. Here, as you walk around
you'll randomly encounter enemies. So, be careful of battles and keep your eye
on your health.

From here, walk to the east and then north to find a bridge across the water.
Then, walk south, around the mountains, and east and south. Then, enter the
Town of Hero.

Walk north and across the bridge, and you'll see the statue that was mentioned
earlier. Pressing A in front of it will let you know that something is missing
from the statue. More specifically, three things are missing from the statue.
You'll be going to track them down shortly.

To the west of the town entrance is the inn. To the east is the House of Life.
To the north you'll find a building with three doors. The left door has a room
with some people inside. In the middle door is an item shop. Here you can buy:

Now, from here there are three places you need to go. You can do the first two

3.03: Castle of Sword

From the town of Hero, head to the west, along the edge of the mountains, until
you come to a path of trees through the mountains. Go through the path, down,
across a bridge, along the path and to another bridge, up through a grassy
area, to the Castle of Sword.

In here, you'll want to avoid talking to anyone you come across, since they're
all enemies that will attack you. From the entrance, take the path on the left.
When you come to a split in the path, go across to the other path and then
continue up and along the path. At the next split go south, around the wall to
the stairs, and up the stairs.

On the second floor, walk north out of the walled area. From there, go around
the side and down, to the bottom of the area, and another staircase.
On the third floor, head up, and you'll come to the King of the Sword Castle.
Talk to him to fight him.

Once you've defeated him, you'll get 240GP. You'll also win the King(Sword).
You can equip this on one of your characters and use it as a weapon. The best
part is, unlike most other weapons which have a limited number of uses, the
King(Sword) can be used an unlimited number of times.

Now, from here head back down and out of the castle, and make your way back to
the town of Hero.

3.04: Castle of Armor

From the town of Hero, head northeast, through the forest, around the mountains
on the top, then back to the west, up and across the bridge over the river. Go
east, to the Castle of Armor.

Inside of the castle are some people walking around, who tell you that the king
wants to speak to you on the second floor. So, go up, through the castle,
across the bridge, and up the stairs to the second floor. Follow the path
around, to a door. In this room is the king. He'll tell you that he's in love,
but that the woman needs some help. So, you offer to help out. Now, walk back
outside of the castle.

Go back to the west and across the bridge. Go east, and then south through the
forest, to a series of rivers with bridges across them. Head over the bridges,
and you'll come to a town. Here, you can heal at the inn, and talk with the
people to get some information. There's also some stores at the north of the
town. The first one sells:

At the back of the town is a House of Life. Also in the back is the girl that
the king wants to marry. Talk to her, and she'll tell you that the reason she
can't marry the king is because a bandit is threatening to destroy the village
unless she marries him. So, now you're off to defeat the bandit.

Leave the town, go down across the bridges, to the west and around the
mountains, and up to a cave. In here, don't talk to anyone, because they'll
attack you if you do. From the entrance, go east, around the stairs and up, and
around the wall to the east. Go south, along the path to a staircase. Go up it
to the next floor.

Go west to the wall, and then south to a door. Go through the door, and then
follow the path to the west and up, where you'll find the bandit. Talk to him
to fight him.

After defeating him, you'll get 120GP. Now, walk back through the cave and
outside. Head back to the town and talk to the woman again, she'll say she's
very happy now.

Head back north, to the Castle of Armor, and talk to the king again. He will
thank you, and give you the King(Armor). Now, head outside and make your way
back to the town of Hero.

3.05: Castle of Shield

The Castle of Shield is directly to the right of the town of Hero, in the
forest.

If you'd came here before doing the other two castles, you would've noticed
that all the guards told you to go away, and that the king and his steward
weren't willing at all to give up the Shield.

Go to the left and up the stairs. Walk forward, and then you'll automatically
walk forward to the king's throne, where he is lying dead with the steward
standing next to him. He'll tell you that he is going to frame you for the
king's murder. You'll then enter into battle with the steward. After defeating
him, you'll see him run into the wall, two spaces to the right of the throne.
Follow him through the wall, to the back of the castle. Go around the wall and
up the stairs. Here, you'll find the steward again. Go over and talk to him to
fight him again. You'll get 400GP and the King(Shield) when you defeat him.
Exit through the back wall to leave the castle.

3.06: Town of Hero/Base Town

If you want to, you can spend some time killing enemies with your new weapon
and armor, since when you decide to move on to the next step you'll be losing
them all.

Now, go to the town of Hero, and unequip all of the King items (and make sure
you equip other items in their place of course). Now, go to the statue in the
middle of the town, stand in front of it, and select the King items from your
inventory menu. As you select them, you'll put them onto the statue. Once all
the pieces are placed on the statue, you'll get the Black Sphere. Start to walk
away, and Gen-Bu will come out and you'll have to fight him. When you defeat
him, you will win 900GP.

Now, make your way over to Base Town. Go up the stairs, and over to the locked
door across the bridge. From your Item menu, select the Sphere option. Then,
use the first sphere in the box. Exit out of the menu, and you can now walk
through the door in front of you.

3.07: Up the Tower (World 1 to 2)

You're now inside of the tower, on the 1st floor (your floor position is listed
in your pause menu). Continue up the stairs and through the next door, and the
2nd floor. Walk south, and then turn west and follow the path to another door.
Go up the stairs, to floor 3. Go west, and you'll see a door and some stairs
up.

Inside of the door, if you want to go exploring, is a small world. There's a
little spot of water in the bottom right which will heal you, and a town in the
top right with some people you can talk to. There's nothing you need to do
here, so, once you're done exploring, you can return to the tower. Go up the
stairs, the west and through the door. Go up the stairs to floor 4. Go north,
west at the first opening, around the wall, to an intersection.

Again not necessary, but if you want to do some more exploring, continue west
to the end of the path and then south down the stairs to a door. Inside of this
door is another side area with some people you can talk to, as well as some
monsters who if you try to talk to them will fight you. Once you're done here,
return to the intersection this path split off of.

Head north, to the door. Here, from the stairs, walk over to the area on the
left to find a treasure chest with a Colt inside. Then, continue up the stairs,
to floor 5. Head south, and you'll find a door, locked by the magic of blue.
This is the door in this area you'll need to open to progress to the next area.
In the southwest corner is a pool that will heal you. Head north and you'll run
into a man, who will give you some information about what to do next. Continue
north, through the door, and out into the next world.

3.09: Caves

From the town, walk north to find a cave entrance. Inside, head off the stairs
and west. Go north to the end of the path, and then east to an intersection. At
the intersection, go north to the end of the path. Go east, past the first
door, to another door. Go through the door and you'll come out on land.

Go to the cave on the other side of the island to enter the caves again. Here,
go down the stairs and then go west, north along the path and follow it east.
Continue east through the intersection, and then take the south path leading
east at the next intersection. Continue east past the next intersection, and
then follow the path north, to a door exiting the caves.

Here, you'll find yourself on land again. On the left side of the land you'll
see two small islands attached to the edge of the land. Go over to the top one
on the left and walk onto it. You'll disappear inside of the island, which
starts floating. You can now use the island to sail over the water.

Again, go around talking to the people in the town to find out some information

3.11: South Island

You don't really need to do this step if you don't want to, it will just give
you some information that you could find out by just skipping to the next step
of this walkthrough. If you want to find it out on your own follow this step.
If not, just skip over to the next section.

Well, that old man the guy in the previous town told you not to see, you'll be
going to see him now. From the East Island Village, get back on your floating
island and head directly south along the east side of the map. Eventually,
you'll come to a small island with a hut on it. Get off of your island and go
into the hut.

Walk along the path, past the first intersection, to the second one. Here, walk
north, past the first intersection to the second one. Here, walk west to the
end of the path. Then, go north to find the old man. Talk to him to find out
about the airseed. Then, make your way back outside.

3.12: Airseed/Underwater Town

Head south, down to the very south end of the map, to an island with a number
of palm trees on it. Stand next to (not on, just next to) the palm tree in the
middle, and, facing it, press A. You'll get the Airseed. (You can actually keep
doing that to get more, but, you only need one).

Now, head back to the north, past the South Island, back up to the East Island.
From the bottom of the island, head west. Eventually, you'll come to a
whirlpool in the middle of the ocean. Move onto it and you'll be taken
underwater. Press A to get off of your island, onto the land. Walk off the
deck, and then go south around the edge of the ocean and then go east. At the
end, you'll find a town. It's also worth noting quickly that you don't need
your Airseed anymore, so, you can just throw it away to free up space in your
inventory.

Here, again, you'll find your usual assortment of people with helpful
information, an inn and a house of life, and a weapon shop. The weapon shop
sells:

Katana(Sword): 50: 2060GP
Coral(Sword): 50: 9880GP
Saber: 50: 2060GP
Silver(Shield): 50: 4000GP
Silver(Gauntlet): 500GP
Book: 20: 500GP
Sleep(Spell): 20: 500GP
Cure: 30: 1000GP

Once you're done in the town, make your way back outside.

3.13: Caves/Sea Palace

From the town, head south to find a stairway. Go onto it to enter the caves. Go
east (ignoring the path to the south of the entrance), to another path leading
south. Follow this path around to come to some stairs. Then, walk east to find
a number of paths leading north. Take the fourth path from the right, to a
stairway leading up to the ground. From there, head south and you'll come to
the Sea Palace.

All of the creatures in here are enemies, so, you'll want to avoid them. Go
east and you'll see a stairway. Head past it for now, and continue to the end
of the path and a door. Go in it and down the path to find a treasure chest
with a Bluekey in it. Then, go back out and down the path, to the stairs.

Go north, and then west down the path. At the end is a door you can get into
using the Bluekey, but, there's nothing inside. Go south, over the bridge, and
continue south and then east along the path to some stairs.

If you want, follow the twisting path going to the north, and at the end is a
door. Go through it using the Bluekey, and inside are three treasure chests.
Inside of them are Silver(Armor), Grenade and Cure(Spell). Then, exit the room
and go back along the path to the south, and at the end is a stairway.

Go along the path, around to the other side of the water and a door, and enter
using the Bluekey.

Inside, walk north. As you go north, you'll see two rows of crystals, one
horizontal and one veritical. Pay attention to where they are in relation to
the room (or don't and I'll just tell you what you need to know). At the top is
a room full of orbs. Pressing A in front of a "fake orb" will cause you to
battle an Atomcrab. Here, stand next to the orb three from the top and three to
the right (you know, where the two lines of orbs from the previous rooms would
intersect), and, facing that orb, press A to get the Red Orb. You'll then enter
into battle with Sei-Ryu. You'll get 4800GP when you defeat him. After the
battle, look at that orb again and press A to be transported to where you first
entered this area at the whirlpool (or you could walk back if you wanted, but,
obviously that would take longer).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.14: South Island/Tower

Head East until you come to the East Island, and then go south to the South
Island. Follow the directions from Section 3.11 to get to the old man if you
need help. Once you get there, he'll ask you a riddle. He'll give you some
items, and ask what item you could buy for the price of the items he listed.
The riddle is random, but the answer will be either a Battle(Sword), Rock or
Needle. Go back to the East Island Village and buy the item you need, and then
bring it to him. Bring it to him and talk to him again, and if you got the
right item he'll reward you by giving you the Blueorb. Now, use either the
Redorb or the Blueorb from your inventory, and they'll combine to give you the
Blue Sphere.

Now, head back to the southwest corner of the ocean, back to to the tower. Go
to the southeast corner of the room, where you'll find the locked door.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.15: Up the Tower (World 2 to 3)

Head up the stairs, to floor 6. Go east, south, then west to a door. Go up the
stairs, to floor 7.

Again, this sidepath isn't necessary, but it's fun. From the door, take either
of the paths leading north, and you'll come to a door. Go in and you'll be on
an island in the middle of the water, but, you can walk in the water. Go north,
through the water, to some grass, and a row of fish statues. Stand on any of
the statues and press A. Some will give you little messages. Others will give
you an XPotion. The ones that give you an XPotion you can press A twice more to
get 2 more XPotions, but if you press A again you'll be attacked. Once you're
done, leave the area and go back to the tower.

From the entrance door, walk west to a door and in. Go up the stairs, to floor
8. Go east, and on the east wall is a pool of water to heal you. Go back west
and up the stairs, past another pool, and then east. Go south and continue
south at the split to a door on the east, and into the door. Go up the stairs,
to floor 9.

Go north, and at the north wall is a door. Inside is a big room, with people in
beds. There's also a person acting as a house of life and another as an inn.
Talk to the people and heal, and when you're done go back through the door.

Go south back to where the entrance door was, then south and down the west
path. Continue past the first split, to the second split going north. Take the
north path, leading to a door. The room on the west has a treasure chest with a
P-Knife in it. Continue up the stairs, to floor 10. Go south, and then east.
Talk to the person standing there, and then continue east. Up the small
stairway to the north is a door locked by the magic of White. Continue east
past that stairway to a wider stairway. Go north, up the stairs, to a door, and
out into the next world.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

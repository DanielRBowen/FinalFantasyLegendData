Gen-Bu and the Three Kings The Hero Statue in Hero Village missing the King's equipment. When the journey begins, the adventurers discover that the Tower is sealed and require a magic Sphere to enter. Exploring the world leads them to discover that the Sphere is hidden away in the Hero's Village. There they learn that the relics of the Great Hero have been taken by three greedy kings who believe that if any were to posses all three pieces, they would be the king of the world. The King of Armor trades his piece when the heroes unite him with a woman he is madly in love with. The King of Sword's is battled to the death for his piece. The King of Shield is assassinated by his Steward who tries to wrap the blame onto the heroes before they chase him down and take the final piece from him. Uniting the pieces with the statue reveals the Sphere, but also summons one of the Four Gods, Gen-Bu who guards it. After defeating him, the party enters the Tower where they find a place where people are punished for their sins by terrible demons and await forgiveness so they can reach Paradise. Sei-Ryu and Ryu-O, The Ocean Kingdom Traveling to Sei-Ryu's castle beneath the ocean Eventually the heroes reach a floor that opens up to a vast world of oceans and small islands. Here they learn that the world was once controlled by a pair of Dragons. The two dragons had a falling out some time in the past and split the Blue Sphere into two before parting ways. Ryu-O took the Blue Orb and moves to the surface world where he vanished from memory. Sei-Ryu took the Red Orb and rules the ocean from his palace beneath the waves. After exploring the islands, the party finds an island that can sail the oceans like a ship and use it to find a mysterious Old Man who gave them a clue needed to find the AirSeed needed to breathe underwater. With it, the party enters the ocean realm and search through Sei-Ryu's castle looking for his half of the Sphere. To counter thieves, Sei-Ryu his the Red Orb among several counterfeits, but the party find the true one and battle Sei-Ryu for it's possession. With the first half of the stone discovered, they return to the surface and speak with the Old Man to find the Blue Orb. He gives them a riddle which they solve and hands them the Blue Orb, revealing that he is Ryu-O in a human disguise. With the completed Blue Sphere, the party climb the Tower again. Byak-Ko and the Sky Kingdom When the party reaches the next world, they find a world among the clouds of perpetual sky. The Sky Kingdom is ruled by Byak-Ko, who runs the place with an iron fist. A Resistance movement has sprouted to overthrow him, but their leader has recently died and his twin daughters now lead it. Byak-Ko is after the White Sphere which will open the door to the higher levels of the Tower, but in order to obtain it, he needs the twin sisters Jeanne and Mileille. Mileille is a prisoner of Byak-Ko and the resistance is led by her sister Jeanne. The heroes enter a Pub that is a hangout for Byak-Ko's men and start a fight, impressed by their skill, the group is asked by Byak-Ko himself to join his forces and help hunt down the Resistance. The party going undercover within Byakko's forces. The group eventually tracks down Jeanne to a small cottage in the woods where she is being apprehended by Byak-Ko's men, after defeating them, the party asks her where her sister is only for Jeanne to explain that Byak-Ko has already captured her. Returning to Byak-Ko's lair, the party rescues her from her cell only for Byak-Ko to capute them, revealing that he already knew the party had slayed his companions Gen-Bu and Sei-Ryu. Mileille is revealed to have also sided with Byak-Ko, believing that only power will prevail. Thrown into the dungeons, the party easily escapes and heads to the Sacred Palace to stop Byak-Ko from obtaining the White Sphere. With Mileille's purpose fulfilled, Byak-Ko tries to dispose of her, but her sister Jeanne takes the blow for her and the party kills Byak-Ko in retaliation. Realizing her mistake, Mileille cries for her dying sister, revealing their tears held the White Sphere all along. Indebted to the party for saving their world from Byak-Ko, the dying Jeanne gives the party the White Sphere. Su-Zaku and the Ruined World Traveling further up the Tower, the party reaches a world that resembles a post-apocalyptic Tokyo. The world is terrorized by Su-Zaku who lays waste to anyone he can find and proves too powerful for the party due to his force field, making them resort to traveling low key through the city's subway. There they meet a girl named Sayaka whom they save from monsters. She leads them to a city with other humans where they meet her brother So-Cho and his biker gang. At first, the gang is hostile but Sayaka explains to them how the party saved her and how they want to defeat Su-Zaku. So-Cho explains how he is building a device to bypass Su-Zaku's forcefield but he still needs a computer board, some rom, and plutonium to make the device. The party volunteers to track down the parts and So-Cho offers them his hoverbike to help them traverse the ruined landscape while avoiding Su-Zaku's constant attacks. The Rom is found in the ruins of an old part of the city which is overrun by monsters. The board is located in another hidden settlement. The ruined cityscape This leaves the plutonium to find which is at the Atomic Reactor. So-Cho and his gang offer to come along to help, but the party tries to sneak out to do it themselves, fearing the ordeal will be too dangerous. So-Cho and Sayaka figure they would try to sneak out and wait for them outside. Finally agreeing to accept their help, So-Cho journeys with the party to the reactor while his sister and the gang protect the base. The reactor proves to be perilous with several monsters and their path way blocked by active radiation rods. When the party finds the reactor core, it is blocked off by radiation rods. So-Cho sacrifices his life to hit the switch to remove the rods giving the party the device needed to stop Su-Zaku as well as his bandanna. While they search the Atomic Reactor for plutonium, Su-Zaku raids the biker hangout and murders everyone but Sayaka, whom he captures and takes to a skyscraper for a final showdown with the party. The party scale the tower and chase Su-Zaku into the basement area where they have their final duel against him on a bullet train. Su-Zaku is confident he will win until the party uses So-Cho's device to destroy his shield and finally defeat him for good. The team reunite with Sayaka at So-Cho's grave where she hands them the Red Sphere that Su-Zaku was guarding. She tells them not to worry about her and thanks them for saving her world. Ashura, The Guardian of the Tower The Tower to Paradise On their way to the top floor, the party encounter Ashura, leader of the Four Gods, who sent his servants to try and stop the party from reaching Paradise. Ashura tries to bargain with the party, tempting them with rulership of one of the four worlds they traveled in exchange for joining him and stopping their pursuit of Paradise. The party refuse his offer and he flies into a rage. After an epic battle, the party emerges victorious. Just as they reach the door to Paradise, a trap door opens beneath them and sends the party back to the base of the Tower. Using the Four Spheres to re-enter the Tower, the party climbs the exterior and encounter the Four Gods again as undead. The Secret of the Tower Once the party reaches the top floor, they enter Paradise to find nothing but a barren room and the Man with the Black Top Hat who had appeared throughout their journey offering advice. He Reveals his true identity as the Creator, who built the tower and created Ashura as a sort of game to entertain himself. Though he offers to give the party whatever they wish, the group refuses after feeling used by him. The Creator brushes it off saying that his creations don't have any sway in his design and they should be grateful to him. The party instead attacks and a climatic battle ensues. With the Creator dead and the mystery of the Tower solved, the party question what they should do. They find one more door in the tower, but instead choose to return to their homes, feeling accomplished for all their hard work.
